Quick intro about myself

Cyber security and information security expert with over 25 years of hands-on experience, specializing in data privacy, security systems implementation, and the secure integration of AI agents into enterprise environments. In recent years I focus on two complementary tracks: Privacy & Regulation — compliance with Israel's Privacy Protection Law and Amendment 13; and AI Integration — the secure deployment of AI-based systems, ensuring sensitive data is not leaked through the model.

  • End-to-end defense — from penetration testing to ongoing operation
  • Regulatory compliance: Privacy Protection Law & Amendment 13
  • AI integration in the enterprise — without leaking sensitive data

Areas of Practice & Expertise

End-to-end defense: from exposure discovery, through security architecture design, to the implementation and ongoing operation of protection systems.

Penetration Testing

Controlled simulation of cyber attacks on systems, applications, code and infrastructure — including AI systems — to find gaps before attackers exploit them.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Design, implementation and management of DLP systems protecting intellectual property, financial information and customer data — preventing intentional or accidental leakage.

Security Architecture

A comprehensive defense strategy: risk analysis, smart security policy, Firewall, VPN, segmentation and alignment with regulatory standards.

Data Privacy & Amendment 13

Guidance toward compliance with Israel's Privacy Protection Law and Amendment 13: gap assessments, data-mapping, security procedures and implementation.

Secure AI Agent Integration

Secure deployment of AI systems and agents — access controls, filtering and data isolation — so sensitive data is never leaked through the model.

Microsoft 365 & Intune Hardening

Identities and conditional access, endpoint management with Intune, information-protection policies and continuous monitoring.
